About

About

Presets made by guitarists, for guitarists.

This site is a small side project. The presets are crafted and tested on real rigs, then shared so anyone with the same hardware can get the same tone without spending hours tweaking knobs.

How it works

Each preset is a snapshot of a complete signal chain — amp model, cab, effects, and EQ. Buy a preset, load it into your unit, and you're ready to play. Demo links let you hear what the preset sounds like before you commit.

Security

You sign in with Google, and the site verifies your ID token's signature against Google's public keys before trusting anything inside it. No password is ever stored.
